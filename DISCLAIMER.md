- Backtests, paper trading, and testnet results do not predict live results.
- You can lose all capital you connect to any exchange or broker.

- Language models hallucinate. DecisionPort outputs are probabilities, not truth. Hard risk limits in code are the control, not prompts.
- RSI-family factors are crowded. Passing Pulsar's gates does not mean the factor will make money.

If you clone this repository, start in paper/sim mode. Do not put live keys on a Grok Bot cloud computer.
